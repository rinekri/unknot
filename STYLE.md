# Style

## One-liner
Hand-painted watercolor storybook with fine ink lines: a quiet, hopeful dawn among floating ruins.

## Style words (paste into EVERY prompt, edits included)
hand-painted watercolor, fine sepia ink outlines, soft paper grain, muted sage and moss palette, warm dawn backlight, pale cream sky, gentle storybook illustration

## Palette
| role | hex | notes |
|---|---|---|
| paper / sky | #f9f3ec | background, fog far color |
| dawn haze | #e9d2b4 | horizon band, near fog |
| stone light | #d5c3a9 | lit rock faces, ruins |
| stone shadow | #645d4f | rock undersides; ink never darker than #342d24 |
| ink dark | #342d24 | outlines, deepest shadow (never pure black) |
| moss | #8f9a4e | grass caps, ivy |
| cloak sage | #5f6f5c | hero cloak only |
| rune glow | #e9b85a | accent: interactables only (runes, totems, goal) |

## Light
- Key light: low warm sun at 2 o'clock behind the subject (backlight), dawn.
- Shadows soft; fill is cool and bright, so shadows stay readable mid-tones.
- Fog: cream (#f9f3ec) by distance; distant islands fade to pale lavender-grey silhouettes.

## Line & surface
- Outlines: yes, thin, sepia ink (#342d24), slightly wobbly.
- Tone: 4-step ramp per material, no specular highlights.
- Texture: watercolor blotches on rock and grass at world scale; paper grain over the whole frame.

## Camera
Third-person, behind and above the hero; FOV 50; height ~3 m, distance ~6 m. Vistas show distant islands against an empty pale sky.

## Reference frames
- `ref/frames/hero.png`: the frame everything is matched against (source still).
- `ref/frames/f_0006.png`–`f_0008.png`: clouds and depth, for sky and fog.
- `ref/video/wanderer_island.mp4`: motion reference (cloak sway, drifting stones).

## Do / Don't
- Do: chunky stone islands with crumbling undersides, small floating debris, ivy and tiny flowers on top, ruins as vertical accents.
- Do: keep the rune-glow gold for things the player can interact with.
- Don't: photoreal surfaces, saturated primaries, pure black, hard rim lights, text in images, busy skies.
